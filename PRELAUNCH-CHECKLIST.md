# Prelaunch Testing Checklist

## Client approvals

- [ ] Jaja approves the design, copy, colors and photographs.
- [ ] Jaja confirms the FAQ and business details in `CLIENT-APPROVALS.md`.
- [ ] Jaja approves the privacy and accessibility wording.
- [ ] Jaja confirms Webby Wahine may continue using the current logo and publicly displayed photographs.

## Content and links

- [ ] Proofread every page.
- [ ] Test every navigation, CTA, email, phone and Instagram link.
- [ ] Confirm all photographs load and have appropriate descriptions.
- [ ] Confirm the strongest image is used in each featured location.
- [ ] Replace Wix-sized images with originals when supplied.

## Form

- [ ] Obtain Jaja’s permission before sending the first test inquiry.
- [ ] Jaja completes the FormSubmit activation email.
- [ ] Confirm a test inquiry arrives at `ladyjajaphotography@gmail.com`.
- [ ] Confirm required fields, error behavior and the thank-you page.
- [ ] Confirm the form does not request sensitive or payment information.

## Responsive and accessibility

- [ ] Test current versions of Chrome, Safari and Firefox.
- [ ] Test desktop, tablet and phone widths.
- [ ] Test the menu, filters, FAQs and lightbox with keyboard only.
- [ ] Confirm focus indicators are visible.
- [ ] Test at 200% zoom and with reduced-motion enabled.
- [ ] Check color contrast and heading order.

## SEO and technical

- [ ] Confirm every title, description, canonical URL and social field.
- [ ] Validate structured data.
- [ ] Confirm `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `404.html`, `_headers` and `_redirects`.
- [ ] Run Lighthouse/PageSpeed checks after deployment.
- [ ] Confirm root and `www` versions resolve to one preferred domain.
- [ ] Confirm HTTPS and redirect behavior.
- [ ] Connect Google Search Console only after client authorization.
- [ ] Do not add analytics or advertising trackers without authorization.

## Migration

- [ ] Export and preserve current DNS records, especially email records.
- [ ] Keep Wix active until the replacement site and form are verified.
- [ ] Test old Wix URLs against the redirect file.
- [ ] Cancel Wix only after Jaja approves the live replacement.
